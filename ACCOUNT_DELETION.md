# HADRA — Account Deletion

**Effective date:** 13 September 2026

You can delete your HADRA account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

## If you use HADRA without an account
Nothing about you is stored in the cloud. To remove your data, clear your history from the **History** or **Profile** screen, or uninstall the app. That deletes everything.

## If you have a HADRA account (email and password)

### How to request deletion
Send an email to **apdizalan68@gmail.com** from the email address registered to your HADRA account, with the subject line:

> **Delete my HADRA account**

You do not need to give a reason. If you cannot email from the registered address, tell us the email address of the account and we may ask you to verify that it is yours before deleting it.

### What happens next
1. We confirm your request by replying to your email.
2. Your account is deleted within **30 days** of the request. You will not be able to sign in once deletion is complete.
3. We send you a final confirmation email when it is done.

## What is deleted
When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:
- Your account (email address, display name, and password hash).
- All reply history and conversation summaries stored in your cloud account.
- Your settings and subscription status.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you use HADRA on more than one device, your cloud data is removed for all of them. Data stored only on a device (such as guest‑mode history) is not affected by account deletion — clear it from the app or uninstall the app to remove it.

## What may be kept
- **Payment records** (such as the date, amount, and reference of a ZAAD, eDahab, or EVC Plus payment) may be retained for as long as required for accounting, tax, or legal purposes. These records are not linked to your deleted account profile.
- Information we are legally required to keep, or that is needed to resolve a dispute or enforce our Terms, for as long as that need exists.

## Active subscriptions
Deleting your account does not automatically refund an active HADRA Premium period. Mobile money subscriptions (ZAAD, eDahab, EVC Plus) do not renew automatically, so no further charges will occur. If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, cancel the subscription in your store account settings to stop future renewals — deleting your HADRA account does not cancel a store subscription.
