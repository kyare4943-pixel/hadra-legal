# HADRA — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 September 2026

HADRA ("HADRA", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps you reply to chat messages. You upload a screenshot of a conversation, and HADRA suggests replies. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using HADRA you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Screenshots you upload
When you upload a screenshot, it is sent to our server so an AI model can read the conversation and write reply suggestions.

- Screenshots are **processed only to generate your replies**. They are **not stored on our servers** after the reply is generated.
- Screenshots are sent to a third‑party AI provider (see Section 4) for the sole purpose of generating your replies.
- By default, screenshots stay only on your device. If you turn on **"Save screenshots in history"** in Settings, a reference to the screenshot is kept in your device's history so you can see it again. The image file itself is never uploaded to your cloud account.

### 1.2 Account information (only if you create an account)
HADRA can be used **without an account** ("Continue without an account"). In that mode, nothing about you is stored in the cloud — your history and settings live only on your device.

If you create an account, we collect:
- **Email address** and a **password**. Your password is stored only as a secure cryptographic hash by our authentication provider; we never see or store your plain‑text password.
- **Display name** (optional).

### 1.3 Your history and settings
For registered accounts, the following is stored in your cloud account so it follows you across devices:
- Reply suggestions HADRA generated for you and a short summary of each conversation.
- Your app settings (language, default reply tone, notification preference, whether to save screenshots in history).
- Your subscription status (Free or Premium).

Each account can only ever read and write its own data. This is enforced at the database level.

### 1.4 Payment information
HADRA Premium subscriptions are paid through mobile money services — **ZAAD, eDahab, and EVC Plus** — or, where available, through the app store on your device.

- Payments are handled entirely by the payment provider. **We never collect or store your mobile money PIN, bank details, or card numbers.**
- We keep a record that a payment was received (amount, date, the phone number or reference the provider gives us) so we can activate and support your subscription.

### 1.5 Technical information
Like most apps, HADRA may automatically collect limited technical information needed to run and improve the service: app version, device type and operating system, language, crash reports, and basic usage events (for example, that a reply was generated). We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Read your screenshot and generate reply suggestions — the core function of the app.
- Create and secure your account, and sign you in.
- Sync your history and settings across your devices (registered accounts).
- Enforce the free daily reply limit and activate Premium subscriptions.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
- Keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix bugs.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your conversations or screenshots to advertise to you.

## 3. Legal basis (where applicable)
Where data‑protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (providing the app), your consent (for optional features such as saving screenshots in history), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, improving the app), and legal obligations.

## 4. Third parties we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run HADRA, and only what they need:

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| **Google (Gemini API)** | Reads your screenshot and generates reply suggestions | The screenshot and your chosen tone/language, sent transiently to generate a reply |
| **Anthropic (Claude API)** | Alternative AI provider that may be used for the same purpose | Same as above |
| **Supabase** | Account authentication and cloud database | Email, hashed password, display name, history, settings, subscription status |
| **Cloudflare** | Hosts our API server | Request data passing through the API (screenshots in transit, not stored) |
| **Expo / EAS** | Builds and delivers app updates | Technical app information |
| **ZAAD, eDahab, EVC Plus, app stores** | Subscription payments | Payment details are handled by the provider, not by us |

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it in countries other than yours (including the United States and the European Union). We choose providers that maintain appropriate security and data‑protection safeguards.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights or safety of HADRA or its users, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (in which case this policy will continue to apply to your data).

## 5. Data retention
- **Screenshots:** not retained on our servers; deleted immediately after your reply is generated.
- **Guest mode data:** stays on your device until you clear history, delete the app, or clear the app's data.
- **Account data:** kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (see our [Account Deletion](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) page), your profile, history, and settings are permanently deleted within 30 days.
- **Payment records:** kept as long as required for accounting and legal purposes.

## 6. Security
We use industry‑standard measures to protect your data: encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic, hashed passwords, database access rules that restrict every account to its own data, and secret keys that are never shipped inside the app. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information.

## 7. Your rights and choices
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can:
- **Use HADRA without an account** — nothing is stored in the cloud.
- **Clear your history** at any time from the History or Profile screen.
- **Change your settings**, including turning off "Save screenshots in history".
- **Delete your account** and all associated data — see [Account Deletion](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md).
- **Contact us** (Section 10) to exercise any other right.

## 8. Children
HADRA is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective date" at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continued use of HADRA after a change means you accept the updated policy.
